Your workbench

Add a shape, then edit its dimensions under Properties. Dimensions and positions use millimetres, with Z pointing up.

Select multiple bodies with their checkboxes, Shift-click, or a long press. For Subtract, select the base first, then its cutters. Source shapes remain editable under the operation.

Use the view cube for Top, Front and Right views. The view menu also offers Back, Left and Bottom. Home restores the 3D angle; Fit frames your model. The left rail includes zoom, orthographic projection, workplane visibility and touch multi-select.

The workplane is 400 × 400 mm. Fine lines are 1 mm apart; major lines are 10 mm apart. Snap Grid changes movement snapping independently. Use Drop to workplane after resizing or rotating a body to place its bottom at Z = 0.

Drag the coloured handles to move, rotate or scale. Drag empty space to orbit; pinch with two fingers to zoom and pan.

W / E / R — move / rotate / scale
F — fit the view
⌘/Ctrl Z — undo
Arrow keys — nudge; Alt+↑/↓ moves vertically

Projects autosave on this device. Save a .bcad file for a portable, editable copy. STL and 3MF are printable meshes. Clearing app/browser data removes local projects.
